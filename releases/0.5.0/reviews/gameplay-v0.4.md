# Independent gameplay review — HOLLOWPACT v0.4.0

**Verdict: approve a preserved v0.4 development milestone only with the newly verified area-attack animation defect explicitly documented and prioritized for immediate v0.5 repair. The reachable Silence save defect is repaired, legacy recovery works through actual controls with original bytes preserved, and tested target previews are truthful and pure. AAA, polished combat presentation, exceptional human enjoyment and commercial Steam promotion are not endorsed.** The v0.3 archive, known defect and independent evidence remain unchanged. World sampling and economy changes are deferred.

2. Restore the exact legacy invalid save without mutating its input. The restored state must equal the repaired legal campaign result, serialize and reload. Recovery must be limited to the known generated guard prefix and exact repeated suffix with correct family/turn, self target, zero damage and empty summons. Invalid HP, card conservation, UID, passive, phase, unknown identity, modified suffix/prefix or oversized content must still be rejected. Already valid saves must follow the normal loader. 3. 4. Production review must wait for the lead's freeze GO and record the actual loaded build digest. Use real controls for complete motion-enabled victory/loss and targeted saved-state fixtures; disclose which is which. Verify recovery notice/resume, preview-to-action agreement, keyboard/target cancellation, harmless late actions and continued optional neutral local reports.
5. Inspect comparative grounded-depth evidence against preserved v0.3 with the same poses, occupancy and actions. Reject feet/shadow/target ambiguity, extra ghost anatomy, hidden silhouettes or measured cadence regressions. Painted 2.5D is an accepted creative direction; full 3D is not a prerequisite. Still images and agent opinions cannot establish human fun or AAA craft.

The strongest existing tactical and progression findings carry forward only where rules remain equivalent. Save reliability must be repaired before a polished-playable claim; historical preservation of v0.3 did not waive this defect. No higher score follows automatically from more tools, screenshots or simulations.

## Craft observations and limits

The production field retains readable target identity and the final-impact pause through full motion runs. Static inspection of the preserved baseline/candidate shadow images shows subtle added floor contact without hiding the viewed silhouettes; the 12-actor candidate remains individually distinguishable in that capture. This is static comparative evidence and actual-control readability, not an independent FPS benchmark or an anatomy/animation verdict. Visual/technical reviews own deeper ghosting, performance and platform acceptance. Painted 2.5D is compatible with the owner's direction and can earn craft on its observed result; a Blender/3D implementation is not required.

All six blank/negative campaign feedback exports preserve actual loaded build/run context, unanswered or negative replay intent, local storage and zero new HTTP requests. A seventh clearly labeled synthetic QA export independently verifies final build identity. These are QA records, not human responses, and must never count toward enjoyment/replay statistics. Feedback wording remains neutral. Sound/mix, controller comfort, native platform installation and prospective-player comprehension were not assessed by this reviewer.

There is one explicit API boundary limitation: adding a cyclic unknown extra field to the invalid legacy object makes `recoverLegacySilenceSave` return a validator-approved object that cannot serialize. I independently reproduced it. Cycles cannot originate from the actual `JSON.parse` localStorage path, which passed the recovery tests. Record a narrow future serialization-boundary hardening task rather than claiming universal arbitrary-object recovery safety or treating this as a demonstrated browser save failure.

## Pre-archive finding: area attack moves its caster offscreen

After the preceding independent play checks, a new production probe exposed a material inherited animation defect. I inspected the exact frozen-build evidence and source, viewed the before/contact/recovery captures, independently recomputed the sprite's transformed bounds, and compared the saved result/events against the expected artifact. This review of the producer's runtime capture is not a new independently executed browser probe or a naturally reached campaign claim.

On the disclosed six-binding diagnostic state, Cindermaw Brood's area breath resolves seven real hits: hunter plus six bindings. The renderer schedules a physical strike per target, then sums every strike's lunge into the same actor's body movement. The caster's entire sprite leaves the 918×457 canvas in **17 recorded draw samples, approximately 222.7–486.8 ms after the action**, and returns by the approximately 603-ms capture. These are actual sampled frame bounds, not an FPS benchmark. The disappearing caster is visibly wrong and weakens source-to-result readability at impact. The archived v0.3 source contains the same cumulative movement and per-hit scheduling, so the evidence does not attribute this defect to the new shadow pass.

The saved state and seven ordered events match the independent expected artifact exactly; there are no page errors. Essential health, intent and target controls remain in HTML. Thus this finding limits presentation quality rather than invalidating the verified save repair, target calculations or completed campaign outcomes. [Runtime evidence](/workspace/Roguelike-deckbuilder/reviews/animation-v0.5/dragon-breath-runtime.json) and [contact capture](/workspace/Roguelike-deckbuilder/reviews/animation-v0.5/dragon-breath-240.png) preserve the fault instead of hiding it.

Historical development preservation is acceptable with this explicit known limitation; I reject promoting the build as polished combat. Repair caster motion once per resolved action/source, rather than adding body displacement for each victim, while retaining all real hit effects and their targets. Dragon breath should communicate a directed area effect instead of a sevenfold melee lunge. The next acceptance comparison must reuse the exact captured state and verify visible, bounded caster movement, seven truthful hits, unchanged save/events, single-target commands, retaliation/mutual death, final-impact settlement and reduced-motion behavior. This repair takes priority before broader world scheduling work.

Tactics, economy and replay structure retain the v0.2/v0.3 strategic baseline; no prices or world schedule changed. Target predictions reduce arithmetic ambiguity and the save repair closes a concrete release blocker. Neither warrants a higher human-fun score. Continue toward separately versioned world sampling, honest prospective-player observation, deeper enemy/event variety and measured craft; keep every negative finding and old artifact.
