# Preliminary policy experiment

This experiment ran while engine development was being completed. Its saved engine/content hashes identify that earlier state, but full source snapshots were not retained. It is preserved as preliminary evidence and must not be treated as a reproducible release qualification. Final milestone experiments retain engine, content and training-script snapshots and reject source changes during execution.

Both heuristic and learned policies won all 32 held-out Standard runs; the learned policy took more turns. The experiment did not demonstrate a gameplay improvement or human fun. Its training objective favors survival/completion; unchanged win rate cannot justify promoting balance changes.
