# Independent gameplay review — HOLLOWPACT v0.3.0

**Verdict: accept v0.3 only as a preserved historical development milestone with an explicit known save defect. The final-impact pause, resolved effects and voluntary local reports pass independent gameplay QA, but repeated legal Silence can invalidate a campaign save. This blocks polished-playable and commercial Steam promotion. AAA craft and exceptional human enjoyment remain unproven.** This is an independent judgment, not required positive consensus.

## Independent investigation before the presentation freeze

I reconstructed nine legally reached first-reward states from preserved runs, covering seeds 6501–6503 and all three difficulties. Each state branches into its three offered rewards and skipping, followed by the same next battle: 36 valid legal branches with common HP, node and route. Reward-versus-skip changes the next enemy family formation in six of nine paired states. In Initiate seed 6502, skipping yields Reaver/Thrall while every offered card yields Reaver/Revenant. Battle setup shuffles the deck before sampling enemies from the shared RNG. A seed label therefore does not hold the world schedule constant when a deck changes. This is evidence for isolating world sampling before interpreting economy treatments as causal deck improvements; it is not proof that every such difference is unfair to a player.

I also reached a concrete lethal-turn state using legal actions: Hunter seed 6501, first battle, turn eight, 5 HP. Reaver's first 6-damage attack kills the hunter; Revenant's announced attack never executes. The inherited arena source scheduled both pre-action intentions, creating a potential phantom attack. This was reported before final freeze. The implementation team replaced intention inference with resolved engine events. Independent direct API and final production browser checks now show only the executed physical effect, with no Revenant magic stroke. Both investigations are retained in [new independent evidence](/workspace/Roguelike-deckbuilder/reviews/gameplay-v0.3-evidence.json).

## Economy proposal review

The engine owner's removal investigation is explicitly counterfactual. It does not patch v0.3. Matched encounter fixtures distinguish inherited dead-card density from access to useful spells; removal alone continues to fail the hardest Cantor fixture, while a generous added spell packet helps. That supports transition access and quarry preparation work, not a blanket Cantor nerf. The 50 heldout reachable pack boss entries all won in original, counter-card and generic-sustain arms. Silence improved mean Cantor health, but the pilot's rescued loss did not generalize into a heldout win difference. A named counter is an option, not automatically the best reward.

Independent audit found terminal hands double-counted in the secondary creature-draw metric. The owner preserved the original harness/results, repaired a separately named revision, and verified all 4,608 primary fixture outcomes unchanged. This does not invalidate the recorded wins, health or procurement outcomes. Creature cards drawn are only dead cards for a policy that refuses to bind them.

Fresh world-sampling controls use archived v0.2 rules in a separately labeled virtual environment. Their prespecified holdout covers seeds 28001–28072, excludes the six pilot seeds and contains 1,728 full runs: four policies, three difficulties and two removal prices. I independently reaggregated the lossless records. All 24 win/health summaries and rescue/new-loss lists match; every key is unique and terminal. All 216 ordinary-pack records and retained traces match exactly between price arms after excluding the treatment label. All 4,510 common-node encounter entries share enemy formations. The harness SHA matches the pre-execution specification: `d6ae4f7fe6f41195ddad5c84ecbe983cf146ad6f32cfac181dfc5785b71f4747`.

## Complete motion-enabled play and outcome recovery

Two complete runs used actual rendered controls, with no game-state injection: Veteran Pack seed 6503 cleared Cindermaw at 41 HP in 189 accepted actions; Veteran Front-focused seed 6501 died in the floor-six elite at zero HP after 99 actions. Every pre-action phase, floor, turn, HP, energy, hand, binding and enemy matched the preserved independent rules trace. Both runs produced zero page errors. Different outcomes exercise successful preparation and a weaker targeting plan; they do not constitute a human difficulty sample.

The ten observed reward/victory/defeat pauses retained a visible field and disabled its inputs while the canonical result was already saved. Measured observation intervals were about 0.74–1.14 seconds; these are automation observations, not precise animation-duration or hardware cadence benchmarks. Final outcome heading focus and explicit settling text communicate the change before the recap. During the brief pause some HP/card values remain those of the preceding field; the outcome text and disabled controls make their presentation-only role clear. Pressing E during both terminal pauses left the saved result unchanged. Replay then produced a fresh same-seed map at floor zero and 65 HP in each case. The [victory impact](/workspace/Roguelike-deckbuilder/reviews/gameplay-v0.3-victory-impact.png), [defeat impact](/workspace/Roguelike-deckbuilder/reviews/gameplay-v0.3-defeat-impact.png) and recap screenshots preserve the actual client.

Four additional checks resume valid states reconstructed solely by legal actions, then click real production controls. In Hunter 6501's reached floor-eight state, a first thrall kills Ash Widow and a later attack falls back to the hunter: the resolved trace, resulting 15→5 HP and visible physical/magic effects agree. The early-lethal case ends at zero with a physical effect and no later Revenant magic effect. Canvas stroke instrumentation records effect colors without changing rules or timing; it does not establish cinematic quality or every pixel's destination. ## Voluntary report review and quality limits

After each complete outcome I exported both a blank report and a deliberately negative report. All four downloads preserve the exact loaded build digest and terminal seed/run/deck context. Default replay intention stays `unanswered`; explicit `no` stays `no`. The neutral question asks which choices felt meaningful or pointless, if any. Exports trigger zero new HTTP requests and leave all localStorage entries unchanged. The visible disclosure accurately says nothing is sent and sharing is the player's choice. Negative synthetic notes explicitly say automated QA, not human playtest data; blank QA exports are identified as QA in the evidence. None may be pooled into human feedback or replay-intention statistics.

This is a useful foundation for honest prospective-player observation. It does not supply that observation. Sound and mix were muted, controller comfort and native platform behavior were not tested by this reviewer, and screenshot/timing checks cannot establish AAA motion, material realism or broad player enjoyment. The limited monster art families, repeated route/event structure and substantial manual commands remain follow-up craft/replay questions. The historical v0.2 tactical score of 7/10, progression score of 6/10 and replay potential of 5/10 remain unchanged because the rules/content are unchanged. The final-impact transition is a demonstrated functional improvement; I do not convert it into a human fun score.

## Newly verified legal-play save defect

The engine owner's broader investigation discovered an inherited, reachable defect after the complete motion runs. I independently replayed all **119 legal campaign actions** for Initiate seed 1989 through both current and archived v0.2 reducers. Every action is legal, every state is byte-identical between versions, and the final state exactly matches the preserved evidence. At Ironjaw's floor-ten turn-three guard, Silence+ and two ordinary Silences grow its intent label from 35 to 62, 89 and finally 116 characters. The third accepted cast crosses the validator's 100-character limit: the preceding state validates, its accepted result does not. A final-production browser recheck resumes the verified predecessor and clicks the third Silence: autosave records 116 characters, reload reports an incompatible save and removes the Resume option. The raw save remains stored. This targeted resumed-fixture check is not a full 119-action browser replay. There were zero page errors. This is a real campaign-save defect even though the two reviewed complete browser paths did not encounter it. It must not be hidden by the successful presentation checks or blamed on virtual economy rules.

[Full legal campaign evidence](/workspace/Roguelike-deckbuilder/reviews/solo-v0.3/silence-overflow-campaign.json.gz) and the independent replay audit in this review's evidence preserve the exact trigger. A narrow next-version repair should make the status label idempotent while retaining the announced armor gain, canceled damage/reinforcements, energy/card costs and next-turn intention reset. Replaying the full campaign, repeating guard-control on Reaver/Ironjaw/Cindermaw, verifying save/reload and ensuring repeated canceled status emits no fictitious additional control event are necessary acceptance checks. Increasing the validator limit would leave the underlying growth defect.

The next priority is this save repair, followed by a separately versioned world-sampling implementation and fresh acquisition/conversion review. Retain the monster-binding identity and readable tactical targets. Avoid changing difficulty or prices solely to make an automated win table more attractive.
