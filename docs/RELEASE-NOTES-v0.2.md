# Hollowpact 0.2 development milestone

The first grim iteration replaces the rejected Lanternbound woodland presentation. Historical v0.1 source, binaries, saves, datasets and independent findings remain preserved.

## Player-visible changes

- A rain-soaked ruined abbey, original detailed monster illustrations and matching portraits replace procedural woodland figures. The arena uses illustrated 2.5D cutouts with directed lunges, traces, summoning and death effects; it does not ship articulated 3D creatures.
- Four creature roles create different command plans: exposed-target hounds, self-recovering stalkers, hunter-protecting colossi and targeted-spell-amplifying spiders. Eight binding variants and sixteen spells each have an enhancement.
- Three forecastable bosses test different answers: Ironjaw's armored retaliation, Hollow Cantor's announced reinforcements and Cindermaw's guard/breath/strike cycle.
- Training now selects the exact deck entry and shows its improvement. Six relics and five shrine choices support deck shaping and recovery.
- The contract interface leaves the central battle clear, shows passive rules, provides enemy inspection and uses layered synthesized combat sounds. Settings and new saves use separate v0.2 storage keys.

## Review-driven repairs

Independent review found and led to fixes for accepted-save title markup, incomplete packaged-build attribution matching, frame-cadence quantization, ambiguous intentions against duplicate bindings stale travel feedback, crowded roster clipping and pending strike coordinates after formation changes. Historical findings remain in their reports alongside recheck results.

33 rules tests and production-browser control/save regressions verify the engine and interface. Independent reviewers used fresh multi-strategy simulations and a complete hard-mode browser playthrough. Linux launch was tested under a virtual display with a cloud-specific sandbox exception; Windows packaging and source correspondence were checked, but native Windows launch remains unverified. See reviews for exact build hashes, methods and limits.

The small learned policy experiment wins 31 of 32 evaluation runs, matching the heuristic rather than improving win rate. It measures synthetic outcomes, not player enjoyment.

## Production verdict and next priorities

This is a development candidate. Reviewers may approve its archive while rejecting AAA/commercial readiness. The production target remains convincing animation, physical combat impact, distinctive hunter presence, richer encounter art and environments, consequential build choices, reliable platform support and observed human enjoyment.

Next cycles should prioritize recorded review findings over indiscriminate content expansion. Prospective-player feedback, target-hardware performance/soak, articulated creature animation, controller access, native installation/update tests and Steamworks/store/rights checks remain necessary. No public Steam release has occurred.
