# Commercial craft benchmark

The owner wants the game to feel as if an AAA studio made it. This is the review target, not a badge applied to a build. Independent reviewers may reject it and must say what they actually inspected. Ratings from agents are not evidence of human enjoyment, broad market appeal or a production budget.

Use major commercial games as craft references: convincing material/anatomy and monster threat; smooth anticipation/impact/recovery; directed spell and command effects; tactile card interaction; coherent lighting/sound; immediate readable intent; clear controller and mouse navigation. These are design expectations, not claims that a footage teardown or benchmark comparison has been measured here.

Required evidence before high-quality promotion:

1. A consistent visual identity across character, card portrait, battle figure, attack effect and description. Complete silhouettes at six-unit occupancy, no clipped atlas anatomy, no contradictory cute art.
2. Every action communicates source, target and result. Summons emerge, attackers anticipate and recover, victims react, defeats remain readable before removal. Standees with scaling/lunges do not qualify as rigged character animation.
3. Restrained environmental movement and sound create tension and physical weight. Different attack types, spells and creature materials need distinct effects; synthesized beeps are a prototype baseline.
4. Essential rules remain legible at supported resolutions, with focus states, motion controls, scale/accessibility settings and clear target changes. Dark atmosphere cannot hide information.
5. Recorded frame-time, load-time and memory behavior on declared target hardware, including a full-run graphics soak. A handful of screenshots or a software-rendered smoke test is insufficient.
6. Meaningful deck decisions, multiple distinct strategies, varied encounters and motivating loss/recovery. Simulated success or content count cannot prove fun. Observe prospective players and preserve their feedback separately from models/agents.
7. Reliable saves and migration, clean-machine install/update, target platform launch, controller/Steam Deck verification where advertised, complete art/audio/code provenance and Steam store/depot review.

For every version, reviewers identify the exact source/build hashes, methods, seeds/platforms and unassessed areas. Report blockers and compared strengths without requiring positive consensus. Freeze, repair, re-review and preserve candidates; protect the last accepted stable build. The absolute promise that a change is never worse cannot be demonstrated; rollback and comparative evidence are the control mechanism.

Independent visual review of actual0.8candidate45bd accepts scoped development preservation and rejects AAA/commercial/Steam quality. The original crypt improves undead encounter identity; complete source/target/terminal cues remain. Sparse pose cuts, repeated silhouettes, mixed outer scenery, mobile control grids, inherited transient full-cell overflow and minor dragon-cell upper overflow remain concrete debt. Sampled cases and agent gameplay checks do not establish human enjoyment, hearing, target-hardware performance or store readiness.
