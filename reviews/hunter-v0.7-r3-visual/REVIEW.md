## Blocking finding and retained limitations

The independent technical reviewer separately measured a blocking repeated-aspect regression, inspected and hash-verified here: resizing a retained dying warleader700×277→390×220→1398×328→502×277→700×277 preserves cue/death identity but irreversibly shrinks119.11px→35.53px; a dying hound similarly shrinks84px→25.05px. R3 multiplies each already-scaled corpse by the new minimum-axis ratio, compounding losses. My single-shrink quad checks do not contradict this defect: contained sprites can still become materially worse.
