## Findings and limits

This replaces generic attack signals with actual-creature/event routing and preserves the tested gameplay contract. It provides a stronger engineering foundation for differentiated feedback; it does not establish that these synthesized sounds are compelling,pleasant or sufficiently material-specific. Listen with prospective players on real hardware before making those claims.

One nonblocking craft debt is visible in source: the39 decoded files include `ui_back`,but production `sound()` always calls `hostAudio.ui()` with its default confirm branch. Distinct back/cancel/reward sonic identity has not been demonstrated by this build. Human hearing,the full campaign mix,hardware output latency/synchronization,spatial mixing and long-run performance remain open. These33 checks are not a full35-browser regression suite or a new release test.

Integrate only the reviewed narrow files/WAVs/provenance,then freeze and independently test the exact combined runtime alongside any hound/environment changes. Native package/security/consumer-install/controller/Deck claims require their corresponding gates. No telemetry or uploaded feedback was introduced by this candidate; I did not test a Steam depot or store workflow.
