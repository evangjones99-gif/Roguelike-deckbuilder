## Actionable priorities

1. **Make final-kill values and labels agree.** The real lethal command at elite decision079 leaves Reaver **4/21HP beside Quarry down** after450ms (080) and screenshot081. Dragon decisions108–110 similarly retain **9/35**, and final victory231–233 retains Thrall **2/11**. Before/after performance.now and exact actions are preserved. The dramatic hold is visible, but positive health undermines its outcome clarity. Held aria labels also retain pre-kill threat descriptions. Preserve the strike presentation while using consistent post-action numbers/status. No surviving-enemy defeat case arose naturally, so I do not claim that behavior was tested here.
2. **Differentiate event choices and encounters.** Both selected shrines repeat all five options; free25gold visibly dominates Leave. First elite is an opening composition plus a duplicated Reaver and higher health. Second elite's summoner and dragon's alternating target patterns produced more distinctive decisions. Improve visible costs/benefits and authored encounter identity before treating global difficulty inflation as progression.
3. **Show earned relic effects at the reward.** Count changes and later names are visible, but I needed public Deck inventory to learn Coin and Nail effects. This matters because opening mana and creature survival influence preparation immediately.
4. **Align creature illustration with its named identity.** In `boss-turn5-choice.jpg`, both Bone Thralls appear as matching fully armored spectral knights rather than visibly bone-built servants. Grim rendered imagery supports tone, but reuse weakens the bestiary and fast threat recognition. This is a screenshot observation, not an asset-rights assertion or an animation approval.
5. **Clarify campaign and victory counters.** Actual victory simultaneously says **You survived ten contracts** and **5 Contracts faced**. Route stages and combat encounters use the same noun for different counts. Rename the counter to Battles fought, or distinguish the route wording. The result is technically coherent only after that distinction is inferred.
6. **Stabilize action confirmation and assess cleanup pacing with humans.** After a card is consumed, stationary pointer guidance immediately describes the replacement card in that slot; the announcer still correctly names the played card. Repeated same-name enemies also renumber after death. Several decisive setups end with a short cleanup turn, including a fourth-turn isolated Reaver. These are clarity/pacing observations, not wrong-card execution or input bugs. Controller/tool thinking and timeout wall time cannot measure human pacing.

## Agent replay judgement and limits

**Maybe.** The preparation choices had concrete payoff: spell relic, target amplification, area training, hunter/group wards and self-healing commands interacted usefully with displayed threat patterns. However, repeated events, repeated armor cleanup and illustration reuse reduce the desire to replay this same content. One informed agent win at one difficulty cannot establish appropriate balance, novice learnability, human enjoyment, long-term variety or AAA quality. The no-casualty outcome offers no evidence about binding-loss emotion or recovery. No native install, physical controller, hearing, accessibility user trial, Steam Deck/Windows hardware or Steam publication was tested. Headless Chromium explicitly mutes audio, so no auditory quality judgement is made.

The win summary and new/replay controls communicated an actual completed campaign. Original retained screenshots show the boss setup, boss death with Thralls still alive, final hold and victory. This report recommends product iteration and future prospective human sessions; it does not approve professional Steam release readiness.
