## API used by separately owned main.ts

arena.ts exports ArenaUnitRegion, ArenaUnitViewport, ArenaReadoutMetrics from battlefield-layout.ts. getUnitRegions() and subscribeUnitRegions() retain their original entry points.

ArenaUnitRegion keeps the inherited moving actual draw-source quad (left/top/width/height), uid, side, slot, feetX/Y, disabled, dead, departing. It adds cell, bodyRect, hitRect, intentRect, inspectRect, identityRect, statsRect, statusRect; every rectangle is absolute canvas-relative CSS pixels. Use hitRect for the primary button: it intersects the actual moving draw quad with the actor's owned body lane and viewport. Use inspectRect for a separate Inspect button. Its identityRect and statsRect are readout subrectangles. intentRect and statusRect are separate siblings. For elements nested in cell, subtract cell.left/top from rectangle coordinates.

readoutUnit is a frozen snapshot, including intent and its cloned summon array. canonicalIndex is the zero-based live canonical side-array index, or null for held/departed UIDs. Legal identity remains uid; slot is a presentation allocation, never an intent-target index. Held UIDs retain their own readout snapshot and slot until collapse ends. Newborn regions do not publish before birth; reused victim slots delay birth until that entire old hold ends. Reclaimable victim slots are filtered against every newer live/unborn owner, so a previous corpse cannot cause a second arrival to reclaim an occupied UID slot.

ArenaUnitViewport includes width, height, canvasAvailable plus requiredHeight, layoutPending, hunterRect. requiredHeight is computed from counts/text rows/width and does not depend on the current CSS height, preventing a height-feedback formula. Main may add measured guidance height separately. layoutPending represents pending allocation or its 320 ms stage, distinct from busyMs(). Regions preserve original presentation-disabled behavior during busy cues; the reducer is the authority. Inspect/read-only availability is main-owned.

setReadoutMetrics({intentRows?,statsRows?}) accepts actual 16 px line-row counts. Supplied measurements replace earlier measurements; actual semantic essentialRows still provide a lower bound and at least 3 intent rows are allocated. Count and row reduction happens only after active cues/birth/collapse holds. Metrics received while busy wait until that hold ends. Measurements are cleared at a new battle. Do not clamp an actual five-row intent to three rows. Multiple wrapped stat rows truthfully increase requiredHeight.

getActionPresentationDelayMs() is the exact nonnegative finite lead for the last accepted playAction, integer ms rounded upward (at most 1 ms quantization); it has no silent hard clamp. Unchanged allocation is 0 unless this action includes an arrival reusing a still-held corpse slot. An expanded allocation waits existing cueBusyMs(), then has a 320 ms allocation stage before its new cues. Ordinary unchanged-formation attacks whose accepted events do not touch a future-born figure start at current renderer time and do not append behind prior unchanged actions. A summon action adds any known reused-slot corpse hold to the common anchor, using max(allocationLead, corpseHold); all of its visual cues and newborn births share that reported anchor. Any accepted event referencing a pre-existing future-born figure as source or target adds that figure's known birth deadline to the same max. There is no silent later birth offset. Root's layoutPending dispatch guard prevents repeated expansion queuing. Audio must use the exact lead. Terminal deadlines must use actual busyMs(), which can exceed the old 1200 ms deadline. Hidden/reduced motion/cancellation settle lead and canonical geometry immediately. Optional presentation failure clears queued/pending presentation and snaps to the accepted canonical state, then rethrows for the host's optional-effects fallback.

## Geometry and lifecycle

Both renderer and HTML consume one published allocation. The full original hunter six source cells, ground anchors, facing and seal source points use one reserved hunter placement; full-cell width is capped within its center band. Creature fitting reserves all unchanged authored pose cells (including crop/source aspect/anchors and recoil/breath), rather than substituting an arbitrary alpha bounding box. Attack and impact origins still use actual shared positions and the existing authored contact-tip movement. Source pixels and pose windows are unchanged.

Dense small widths use up to two rows of three on each side. Two continuous width phases move columns before folding rows, instead of a 960/961 threshold jump. Sparse sides retain their larger budget. Width/count/text configuration selects the same cells for paint and DOM. Base dense360 geometry is 634 px; five intent rows yields 698 px with one stats row. This is a source design budget, not a measured text guarantee; real wrap rows can legitimately require more height.

Allocation growth retains every existing UID slot, including upcoming victims. Previously held deaths finish before growth commits. Count reductions and canonical reindexing wait for all cues, births, and collapse holds. Count or slot changes commit atomically inside the allocation stage. They do not interpolate through other owners. Same-count, same-slot metrics changes retain the prior interpolation, subject to the later swept-envelope review. Resize retains cue/impact/hold/waiter clocks and the original absolute min-axis death-size basis; source formulas remain continuous but intermediate and interrupted resize contexts still require actual checks.

## Rejected interpolation research and remaining gates

REJECTED-INTERPOLATED-ALLOCATION.txt preserves the immediately preceding interpolation function. Source arithmetic found that 6-to-5 reindexing can move a lower-row survivor through an upper-row readout. For a 360 px 2-to-3 binding change, the right body can sweep into the hunter's previous center band despite disjoint endpoints. The freeze therefore commits count/slot geometry atomically after hold. This visibly snaps a count reflow; no animation-polish claim is made.

Actual compiled integration, canonical action/terminal/fallback behavior, label glyph wrap, hit ownership during movement, mixed counts 1–6 across widths, hunter full-cell clearance, resize during active cues and metric reflow, keyboard/controller/focus, reduced motion/visibility and audio deadlines remain required. Safe endpoint rectangles alone are not a swept-envelope proof. Old rejected R2 and frozen R1 have not been promoted or edited.
