# Owner playtest intake: cohesive interaction overhaul

The owner review is human evidence of weak appeal, clarity, immersion and combat feel. It does not identify an exact build, platform or seed, and is not a quantitative enjoyment label. Its full original text is preserved unchanged. Existing agent development approvals do not overrule it or establish AAA quality.

The central problem is divided visual and interaction authority: the illustrated battle is a backdrop while duplicate side rosters carry commands, targets and intents. The redesign should make the battlefield the primary command surface, with compact health/attack/intent information attached to actual figures and accessible DOM controls aligned to their renderer geometry. Keep inspection secondary. A missing-art/canvas fallback must remain fully playable and truthfully labelled.

Use one canonical geometry contract for painted figures and interactive controls. Hit areas must not drift after resize or animated formation changes. Explicit selected/valid/invalid cues and pure-reducer consequence previews should remain visible before commitment. Ready commands, spent commands, lethal hits and retaliation need distinct readable feedback. No hidden new placement rules: summons currently enter the first free engine slot; a highlighted destination should reflect that actual slot rather than asking for a meaningless choice.

The hand belongs below this battle space. Cards should lift/enlarge on hover or focus, expose cost and rules clearly, and retain keyboard/controller/touch equivalents. A summon destination cue and a targeting connection should make actions legible. Any drag interaction must commit only a currently legal action at a valid release; release outside, Escape, lost capture, modal opening and presentation lock must cancel safely without changing saved state.

The entry flow should reduce reading and unnecessary confirmations. Show a concise hunter invitation and a small set of meaningful preparation options with optional details. A single forced route should not demand an extra choice. Preserve intentional saved-run replacement confirmation and reproducible seed/replay controls. Remove combat-oriented title clutter while retaining discoverable settings, help and optional local field reports.

The supplied reference contributes only structural cues: large grounded figures in a coherent lit environment, clear opposing sides, tangible cards related to the creatures and restrained supporting decoration. Its warm tavern/castle mood, exact layout and franchise-adjacent identities are not targets. Compare a grim painted 2.5D treatment with a sharper stylized dark-fantasy treatment using the same encounter and information; choose on readability, identity, animation feasibility and actual independent assessment. Anime was suggested as a possible avenue, not mandated. Art alone cannot repair disconnected controls.

Work order: direct battlefield command/target surface and concise entry; tactile hand and valid destination/target previews; grounded animation and attack timing/audio; cohesive art and card frames. Existing fe3f animation and expanded preparation5580 remain research baselines, not automatic promotions. In particular, more preparation text must not reproduce the owner's reading-burden complaint.

Acceptance requires actual playable comparisons against retained801db, independent gameplay/visual/technical reviewers allowed to reject, current-source/build hashes, responsive layouts, keyboard/controller adapter paths, reduced motion, missing-art fallback, reload/save consistency and meaningful combat scenarios. Agent wins are balance evidence, not human fun. Do not publish a milestone solely for styling activity; keep improving after accepted checkpoints.
