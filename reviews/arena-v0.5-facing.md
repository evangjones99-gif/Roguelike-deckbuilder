## Change and continuity policy

Each figure now owns presentation-only facing, initially enemy left / ally right. The latest actual physical gesture's reconciled source/target horizontal direction controls the painted mirror throughout anticipation, attack and recovery. A neutral horizontal displacement ≤max(4px, 4% of figure size) retains its prior direction. Reaction retains facing without inventing a new physical command. A living idle returns deliberately to its original side profile; this is a cel change, not a smooth turn animation. Death retains the last orientation through gesture expiry and disappearance. Magic area casts and retaliation cues never invent a new body gesture or redirect facing.

No image, event, damage, RNG, state/save schema or movement coefficient changed. ## Remaining craft limits

This is painted 2.5D cel motion, not rigged anatomy. Sparse six-pose hard changes and the deliberate living-idle return remain visible. Mirror correction does not solve every extreme-column/diagonal/near-vertical contact gap: fixed .74 body displacement and a fixed painted weapon reach can separate tip and impact under large formation distances. Independent review must assess those cases before promotion; source-owner measurements here do not assert universal authored contact. Additional painted turn/inbetween frames and a separately reviewed reach-aware trajectory remain possible future improvements. No human fun/AAA/Steam readiness claim follows from these probes.
