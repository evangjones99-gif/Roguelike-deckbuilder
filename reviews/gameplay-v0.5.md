## Known gaps and required next work

The actualcdf and immutablev0.4 browser independently reproduce two inherited keyboard gaps. Killing a nonfinal focused enemy leaves focus on `BODY`, despite combat continuing with five enemies. A held `Enter` produces a repeat activation that advances from selecting a binding to actually commanding the automatically focused enemy. Separate intentional keypresses work; held activation can commit an unintended irreversible combat decision. These are concrete accessibility/intentional-input issues, not a fabricated new RNG regression. Retain them explicitly and repair/test them immediately in the next milestone; public polished-input approval is withheld.

The separate visual reviewer found the new warleader could face away from a rightward target. Parent source investigation also found malformed settings could prevent a valid saved campaign from reaching the Resume loader. These were genuine promotion blockers and are now repaired and independently accepted on d622 as detailed above. This reviewer attempted an independentcdf malformed-settings probe, but the strict build guard refused the already superseding997b candidate, so there is no invented independentcdf settings observation. The final repair acceptance is an actual four-case production probe, separately qualified from that parent finding.

After those repairs: prioritize activation latching/focus recovery and actual controller traversal, then matched-world procurement investigations and first-run observation by real people. Preserve new losses and negative/“none” feedback. More authored animation, distinctive threats/environments and a visible hunter remain craft needs. This review does not certify native packaging, Steamworks/depot installation, platform hardware, rights or commercial completeness.

Reviewer tooling corrections are preserved: the campaign and final-context initialization helpers initially reinjected their starting fixture on reload, then were guarded to initialize only an absent primary save. Their failed snapshots and repair metadata remain alongside successful evidence; this was reviewer setup, not a runtime save defect. The candidate diagnostic guard also refused an unannounced build transition rather than combining digests. No frozen engine evidence was rewritten.

Next-cycle [independent input acceptance criteria](gameplay-v0.5-runtime/next-input-review-criteria.json) were prepared during this review. They require explicit press/release activation, meaningful focus after removed cards/units and terminal transitions, text-field/modal behavior, controller disconnect/held-button checks and exact rule/save comparisons. Synthetic gamepad testing must remain separate from native hardware or Steam Input certification. This milestone is a preserved checkpoint for continued production, not a declaration that the game is finished.
