Each new `installer-research-run-ID` lightweight tag targets the exact historical source SHA and has a clearly failed draft prerelease. A foreign/orphan existing tag, a published or edited draft, an unexpected/duplicate/changed asset, or a wrong digest fails closed. New assets are whole original NSIS, original small CI evidence ZIP and a deterministic coverage receipt—three per draft, nine maximum.
