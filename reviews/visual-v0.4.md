### Late finding before preservation: multi-target breath displaces its caster off screen

It must not be presented as approval of this animation or a release suitable for customers. The finding narrows the earlier accepted visual milestone: ordinary hound motion improved, but multi-target attack presentation is materially broken.

I inspected the arena author's actual frozen-production diagnostic evidence in `reviews/animation-v0.5/dragon-breath-runtime.json`, plus the before/180/240/600ms completed-canvas PNGs and the reproduction script.
