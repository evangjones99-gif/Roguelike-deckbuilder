## Compared visual judgment

The crypt now continues behind the side panels and guidance rather than revealing the unrelated courtyard. This improves location coherence without removing the intentional rectangular arena crop seam. Dark material and lights support the original gritty hunter direction. Victory retains the same encounter location and the returned title preserves its abbey image.

At equal-age contact the lower jaw closes while the painted upper nose, torso and paws retain their shape. At the inspected actual sizes neither facing shows an obvious duplicate open jaw or forearm pull. The change is modest: a few visible pixels on mobile, clearer on desktop. Anticipation/contact/recovery and hound death remain readable. This is a useful anatomical articulation, not a fully animated creature or proof that every opacity/facing/hardware combination is perfect. No independent partial-opacity helper stress or native file-URI launch was run in this visual gate.

The fault views retain controls and distinguish unavailable illustration from partial missing art. The unavailable-Canvas notice is still plain, uncontained serif text; the missing-art notice is very small. These inherited presentation weaknesses merit a designed status panel and accessibility review. No WCAG certification follows.

Known craft debt remains visible: final-held enemy bars can read `1/31` beside `Quarry down`; scene/jaw does not fix that existing readout defect. The source correction is separately under review. Victory still says ten contracts while its combat counter says Contracts faced; the diagnostic boss-entry fixture makes the semantic mismatch especially visible, but it is not evidence of a real one-battle campaign. Mobile six-unit figures are very small, many creatures still share portraits and standee motion, lighting/crop seams remain, and stronger full-body animation/variety is needed for the AAA target.

## Retained failure and limits

Initial scene harness R1 completed14 contexts, then wrongly tried Resume after reloading a terminal victory save. Its30-second selector timeout, log, incomplete-context exclusion and all14 completed result rows are retained in SCENE-FAILURE.json.gz. It was a reviewer harness mistake: terminal saves intentionally have no Resume. R2 performs fresh candidate/baseline boss actions, inspects settled victory, returns home, reloads title and asserts Resume absence. Both R2 contexts pass. The incomplete R1 boss context is excluded from successful-context/action totals. Candidate/source/art bytes were not changed to make this pass.

All own browsers and the own baseline server were closed before freezing. These are browser observations, not packaged-native, audible listening, physical controller/Steam Deck, target-hardware performance, external player enjoyment, title/rights clearance or Steam-release evidence. Separate technical/native/other gameplay gates remain required before broader promotion. The source/output audit is provenance verification, not a fresh compiler reproduction.
